Simulation Summary

At a Glance

Both columns were simulated in HYSYS with identical feed conditions (100 kgmole/h, 78°C, 101.3 kPa) and the same reflux ratio of 2.0, targeting an ethanol distillate purity of 90 mol%. The columns differ in their internal geometry, pressure drop behaviour, and sizing requirements.
